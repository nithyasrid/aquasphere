AquaCheck

What can you observe?

No rainfall measurements. No technical formulas. Answer simple questions about soil, rain, plants, water sources and water use.

01. What have you noticed about the soil?

Think about soil around your home, garden or neighborhood.

02. What happens when it rains heavily?

Think about what you see after strong rainfall.

03. What are nearby plants doing?

Look at trees, grass, gardens or other vegetation.

04. What is happening to a local water source?

This could be a well, pond, tank or familiar source.

05. Has water use around you changed?

Think about your home, neighborhood or farm.
